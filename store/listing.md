# Play Store listing: Akshara Aata

**App name (30 chars max):** ಅಕ್ಷರ ಆಟ - Kannada Alphabet

**Short description (80 chars max):**
Kannada varnamala for kids: real voices, writing checker, stories & 8 fun games

**Full description:**

ಅಕ್ಷರ ಆಟ (Akshara Aata) helps young children learn the Kannada alphabet by listening, tracing and playing.

ALL 49 LETTERS
• 13 ಸ್ವರಗಳು (vowels), 2 ಯೋಗವಾಹಗಳು and 34 ವ್ಯಂಜನಗಳು (consonants), grouped by ವರ್ಗ
• Every letter is spoken by a native Kannada speaker and comes with picture words and a little line: ಆ – ಆನೆ 🐘, ನ – ನವಿಲು 🦚
• ಕಾಗುಣಿತ: see, hear and write ಕ ಕಾ ಕಿ ಕೀ … for every consonant
• Kannada numbers ೦ to ೧೦ with counting

WRITE WITH A FINGER
• A crayon shows the right stroke order for every letter and kagunita
• Trace each letter on copybook lines with colourful crayons
• Write from memory: the handwriting checker recognises the letter ("that looks like ಬ!") and coaches where to start

SMART PRACTICE WITH AANE 🐘
• Aane the elephant plans 5 letters a day: hear it, write it, say it
• Letters your child finds hard come back sooner (spaced repetition)
• A new read-along story every day when online; built-in stories offline

8 FUN GAMES
• ಕೇಳಿ ಹುಡುಕು: hear a letter and find it
• ಚಿತ್ರ ಆಟ: which letter does the picture start with?
• ಜೋಡಿ ಆಟ: match letters with pictures
• ಬಲೂನ್ ಆಟ: pop the balloons with the right letter
• ಮುಂದೆ ಏನು?: fill in the missing letter
• ಕಾಗುಣಿತ ಆಟ: ಕ + ಇ = ?
• ಎಣಿಸು: count and pick the Kannada number
• ಹೇಳು ಆಟ: say the picture word and the phone checks it

MADE FOR KIDS AND PARENTS
• Stars and stickers keep children motivated
• Progress tracking for parents
• Settings are behind a parent check
• No in-app purchases, no accounts
• Everything works offline; the handwriting checker and smart practice run on the phone
